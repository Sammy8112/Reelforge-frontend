Pipeline

Three steps between idea and export.

No timeline to learn. No footage to shoot. The model does the coverage; you do the direction.

01 — Direct

Describe the shot

Write what you want in plain language, or drop in a reference image. Camera move, lighting, mood — all in the sentence.

02 — Render

Model does the coverage

Your prompt routes to the video model best suited to it — photoreal, animated, or product-clean — and renders in the background.

03 — Export

Pull it into your edit

Download in the aspect ratio you need, or send straight to your usual editor. No watermark on paid renders.
